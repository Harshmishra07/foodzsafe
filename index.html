Our Services

We offer comprehensive Quality, Food Safety and Compliance solutions customised to the needs of the food & beverages industry.

Food Safety Management System (FSMS) Implementation

A Food Safety Management System (FSMS) is a structured, preventive framework designed to control various food safety hazards across the food supply chain. It helps food businesses protect public health, ensure legal compliance, and maintain consistent product safety.

ISO 9001 (QMS), ISO 14001(EMS) & ISO 45001 (OHSMS) system implementation

A QHSE management system combines Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment into one integrated management system. It helps companies protect workers, care for nature, and make quality products. Core Pillars
• Quality (ISO 9001): Meeting customer needs and improving work tasks. • Health and Safety (ISO 45001): Preventing workplace injuries and illnesses.
• Environment (ISO 14001): Helps in reducing waste and lowering pollution

HACCP Plan Development, Validation & Verification

HACCP stands for Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point. It is a smart safety system that prevents food safety hazards before them entering into food supply chain based on 7 principles by Codex Alimentarius Commission. Validation of HACCP plan on the basis of scientific evidences before implementing. Verification of HACCP plan after implementation that plan is actually working as intended.

Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) & Good Hygiene Practices (GHP) Compliance

GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) and GHK (often referred to as GHP, or Good Hygiene/Hygienic Practices) are core operational standards used in manufacturing and food products supply chain. GMP focuses on how products are safely made through process control, while GHP/GHK focuses on hygiene, sanitation, and cleanliness of the environment and staff.

Internal Audits & Gap Assessments

Internal audits and gap assessments are essential tools to measure performance, ensure compliance, and drive continuous improvement. A gap assessment identifies what is missing compared to a standard or requirements, an internal audit tests if implemented controls work effectively, and together they form a complete compliance roadmap, e.g. Gap Assessment or Internal audit against any of the food safety management system standard.

Regulatory (FSSAI/USFDA-FSMA) Compliance Support

Regulatory food compliance involves standards set by authorities like FSSAI , USFDA, and FSMA to ensure food safety, proper labelling, and public health protection. FSSAI governs domestic food supply chain within India, while the USFDA and its FSMA rules regulate safety and prevention for food imported into or sold within the United States.

FSSC 22000, BRCGS, IFS, SQF & Other GFSI-Recognized Food Safety Standards

The Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI) harmonizes global food safety management through benchmarked schemes, with FSSC 22000, BRCGS, IFS, and SQF serving as the four dominant global standards. While their food safety outcomes are mutually equivalent, they differ in structure, origin, and regional market preferences.

Employee Training & Capacity Building

Employee training and capacity building is a planned process to improve worker skills, knowledge, and team systems. We help boost job performance, supports daily tasks, and prepares a workforce for future challenges and creating positive quality and food safety culture within organization. Key Goals and Focus Areas
• Skill Enhancement: Training on specific job tools, technology, and daily tasks.
• System Growth: Improving internal processes, communication, and team structure.
• Future Readiness: Preparing staff for new industry trends and leadership roles.

Supplier Audits & Vendor Qualification

Supplier Audit and Vendor Qualification form the core framework used by many organizations to evaluate, approve, and monitor third-party suppliers of Raw materials/Packaging Materials & Service Providers. Vendor qualification is the overarching systematic process of onboarding suppliers based on risk, capability, and compliance, whereas a Vendor Audit is a targeted inspection tool used within that framework to verify actual site practices and quality systems. We can provide supplier/vendor audit services on behalf of our clients.

Documentation, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Risk Assessments

Risk Assessment, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and documentation form the core framework for safe, repeatable, and high-quality operations for any Food Safety & QHSE standard implementation. They provide clear instructions, reduce errors, and protect product, people or assets by addressing potential hazards before work begins. We help our clients for preparing required Risk Assessment & other documentation for implemented standards like QHSE, FSMS, BRCGS, IFS etc. within the company
